## What is Image to Video AI?

**Image to Video AI** is an **open-source tool** designed to transform your images into high-quality videos. Unlike traditional video editing software, this AI-powered solution simplifies the process, making it accessible to everyone. With its advanced algorithms, **Image to Video AI** can create videos with smooth transitions, stunning visuals, and professional-quality effects. Whether you’re merging two images or generating a full video, this tool offers unparalleled versatility.

## Why Choose Image to Video AI?

There are several reasons why **Image to Video AI** stands out in the crowded market of video creation tools.
